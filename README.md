# hyperfocus.tech

## Autonomous ops team for e-commerce brands and the agencies that run them.

We connect your sales channels and run your e-commerce operations autonomously — you approve decisions, agents do the work.

Agents run fees, creators, inventory, ads and compliance across TikTok Shop, Amazon, and Shopify. You set targets and guardrails — agents execute.

---

### What the agents do

- **Fee Auditing** — audit every settlement across all channels. Catch overcharges across Amazon's 47+ fee types and TikTok Shop's 15 fee types, surface discrepancies, file disputes automatically.
- **Creator & Affiliate Management** — track creator ROI including samples sent. Detect ghosting. Manage commissions, outreach, and performance at scale.
- **Inventory Automation** — predict stockouts based on sales velocity and supplier lead times. Trigger restock alerts before best-sellers run out.
- **Ad Spend Optimization** — reconcile ad spend from Meta, TikTok, and Google Ads to actual sales. Calculate true ROAS at the product level.
- **Compliance Monitoring** — track SPS scores, late delivery rates, cancellation rates. Escalate before thresholds are breached.
- **Settlement Reconciliation** — match every order to every fee to every payout. Surface discrepancies between platform reports and bank deposits.
- **Campaign Coordination** — coordinate promotions, flash sales, and seasonal campaigns across channels.
- **Growth Intelligence** — surface anomalies, market shifts, and opportunities from operational data.
- **Portfolio Management** — unified view across all client brands for agencies. Scale clients without scaling headcount.
